About

👋 Hey there!

I’m currently a Machine Learning Engineer at ByteDance. My work focuses on building agents and the harnesses around them.

I received my M.S. from Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), where I worked with Prof. Min Xu at XuLab. Before that, I completed my undergraduate studies at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) and worked with Prof. Ziwei Liu in S-Lab. I also continue to collaborate with Dr. Liang Pan on several research projects.

My research interests include 3D generation and world models.

Outside of work, I enjoy singing—skill level aside—and trying out new hobbies. My recent experiments include Black Myth: Wukong, guitar, and indoor bouldering.

I also have an especially cute dog named Mocha. You can find more of Mocha on Instagram.

I’m always up for an interesting conversation, a brainstorming session, or a potential collaboration—feel free to connect!
